THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROFESSION

My interest in business did not begin in a lecture room. It began much earlier, through the everyday experiences of growing up.

Spending time with my grandmother and hawking exposed me, at a young age, to the practical side of buying, selling, customers and money. I began to notice that every small business had a story: a product, a customer, a cost, a decision and a result.

That experience planted an early interest in business. But as I grew older, my curiosity took a different direction. I became interested in accounting because I wanted to understand what happened behind the scenes — how businesses operated, how transactions were recorded and how the numbers in a book explained the reality of a business.

Accounting became more than a course of study. It became a way to look beneath the surface, connect activity to performance and understand the decisions that keep organisations moving.

“I didn't just want to see a business from the outside. I wanted to understand how it worked from the inside.”

Today, that curiosity continues to shape my journey in finance. The tools and responsibilities have changed, but the question that drives me remains the same: what do the numbers tell us about the business?
